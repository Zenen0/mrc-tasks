## Rationality

Basic rationale tells us one is rewarded only according to true efforts put in. For the worldwide trader the only tool needed is a strong sense of rationality. It is also the most commonly distorted - going right back to deeply ingrained misinformed beliefs that require an innate subconscious lapse of rational thinking to accept (embedded misinformation that stems from society, environment, media, negative habits and targeted distractions).

All other branches related to psychology fall under it. All deeper truths discerned via rationality. Hence, its importance is mentioned first. A concept one must familiarize self with. Only from it can the mind be free from all cognitive bias.

A few examples:

- Why would the trader recklessly trade live and risk hard-earned money (why be so free to let banks beat you twice?) Relying purely on high leverages, overtrading, accepting lack of consolidated knowledge - prior to seeing some kind of confident daily consistency? There should be no room left for doubts in ability - unless it is a lapse in rational thinking.
- How does one wish to earn amounts people do not see in a year in a day or sometimes minutes and not give full focus with every fiber of their being towards hours of backtesting? Many here, upon questioning, have not given the minimum 500 hours (with proven data to show for it) - a lapse in rationality.
- The search for truth is the essence of life. Why then does one allow self to be distracted by trivial affairs instead of engaging in continuous learning and reflection? Certain forces do not want the masses to be free thinkers or united. But all their attempts at programming do not penetrate the mind strong in rationality.
- Before thinking in probabilities comes thinking rationality.
- Chapter 3 of *Trading in the Zone*: Taking responsibility. Taking responsibility is only done via rationality. So is every other concept linked thus.

Risk management, trusting the system when in profitable trades, taking advantage of % compound while accounting for mental wear, hours spent in studies, deeper perceptive fundamental studies, and knowing the deeper purpose of liquidity - all require rational thinking.

With the system provided (and that reverse-engineered using rationality, expressed in algebra), nothing else is needed. Not a deposit power, nor a previous education bar comprehension skills (fixed via extra efforts).

The innate gift of mental clarity anyone can harness is based on forging will power via true actions.

## Data storage obligation

One must store all your backtesting data - it is an obligation. However it does not count if it is here on Discord only. Printed charts, organised files, case studies, sticking charts on walls, your own backtesting "libraries" - make it tailored to you. Show yourself your efforts and your focused energy building. Backtesting does not count without stored data (unless one is watching charts live - and then hours spent is the proof of results).
