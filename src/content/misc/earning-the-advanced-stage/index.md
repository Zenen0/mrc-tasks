## What the advanced stage is

The advanced stage is a separate, invite-only step beyond the current team material. Mr Casino describes its purpose as holding "the most profound trading knowledge in the most clear format - every explanation will be shared right to core, at the most mechanical level."

He keeps it deliberately quiet: "Its worth is great and its security most paramount. The less spoken of the more secure." Only "the most worthy, capable, trusted minds" are included, and those chosen do not speak of their participation publicly. **"The only mention will be the increased results seen."**

*Later, in the [Segments](/mrc-tasks/tasks/segments/):* Segment 2 notes that "Serious learners will extract the meaning from the key themes discussed only through the repetition", with the third part "to be privately shared". Segment 3 ends by pointing to a "future (locked) final advanced segement". He does not say whether that segment is this stage; it remains an open thread ([Segment 3 · Still to come](/mrc-tasks/tasks/segments/3/assignment/#still-to-come)).

## The criteria

The progression criteria, in his words:

> Must complete all backtesting tasks, engage, show passion and a positive free mind of constant improvement - assuredly you will be included eventually.

- **Complete all backtesting tasks.** Every task, not a selection. See [Rationality & Backtesting Discipline](/mrc-tasks/misc/rationality-and-backtesting-discipline/) for why backtesting only counts with stored data.
- **Engage.** Take part in the team, not just read it.
- **Show passion.**
- **Keep a positive, free mind of constant improvement.**

Meeting them is the path: "assuredly you will be included eventually." Inclusion is earned, not requested.

## Following instructions is part of the test

The announcement itself was set "as a sort of test in comprehension skills":

> It is certainly needed for that advanced trader especially for this highest calibre of learning. How well is one able to follow instructions?

The instructions attached to it:

- **Don't ask how to join**, and don't speak publicly about not being included.
- Read the message carefully: "Do read this multiple times, Translate it to your native language if you must."
- Speaking about it does not disqualify anyone, "but you increase your chances by proving yourself able to follow instructions given."

He is "on the lookout for new additions who prove themselves capable."

## Value what is already shared

> Majority of what will be mentioned is already here In the server If only albeit more scattered.

The advanced stage "will only solidify knowledge and complete application, with some unique outlook". The material needed to progress is already available, only less organised. Chasing the next stage instead of working through that material is its own warning sign:

> Constantly asking regarding it means one does not value what is shared already and harbours self doubt at ability.

This ties back to the self-reliance points in [Morality, Rationality & the Opposing Side](/mrc-tasks/misc/morality-rationality-and-the-opposing-side/) ("Everything you need is already provided") and to trusting your own ability rather than waiting for more.

## What is being watched

He follows "the progress, efforts, focus and how true one is in utilization of the already provided intensive contents". **"One will be included when ready."** In practice that means:

- completed tasks with data to show for them;
- consistent focus and effort over time;
- genuine use of what has already been taught;
- the ability to follow instructions exactly.
