## The model, step by step

1. **The banking system is the leading power.** In the mentor's view, the fiat USD is "backed by nothing fundamentally" apart from never-ending debt to draw on, and ordinary citizens feel the economic repercussions.
2. **Control is incomplete.** Controlling the world reserve currency, the base of all transactions and especially of gold buying and selling, did not give a clean sweep. Russia, China and the Arab nations hold their own resources and physical gold and want to end the one-sided monopoly.
3. **Gold price control keeps the system afloat.** "The real fundamentals of the markets, stemming from XAU/USD." Rising spending weakens confidence in the USD through inflation. Competitors, China above all, can offer their own fundamentals-based gold price, and a higher price attracts investors and more physical gold accumulation. So the price has to be constantly adjusted ("manipulated") to allow only "completely accounted for changes".
4. **It all comes down to liquidity.** Fundamentals ultimately act on the physical supply of and demand for gold. If the banks don't move their fixing of XAU/USD to match physical demand in due time, other major countries and players win.

## What the banks do

- "Banks are always short-selling only to buy again at some later date."
- They would like to take price down forever but can't because of competition. Their role is to **manage the required price level** of gold and profit greatly while doing it.
- Their profit comes from controlling all bid and ask prices, with complete access to the exact order flow (the liquidity on both sides), enhanced with AI to target at the most macro level.

## Their limit, and why it matters to us

> There is an extent they are comfortable with before fundamental factors, namely for buys, overpower.

The banks can hold gold down only so far before fundamental buying pressure overrides them.

- The general day trader can't use this knowledge because they can't view the market "from the true lens of RR extraction".
- For us, it is **"the last step in securing the bank's complete footprint"**, and it supports **full swing confident holds**.
- The aim is to see the banks' "printing press of all the markets stemming from USD and then stemming from XAU control exactly as they do."
