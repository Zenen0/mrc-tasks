## The model, step by step

The model below is Mr Casino's view of how the monetary system works. It is presented as his framework, not as established fact.

1. **The banking system is the leading power.** The fiat USD is "backed by nothing fundamentally" apart from never-ending debt to draw on, and ordinary citizens feel the economic repercussions.
2. **Control is incomplete.** Controlling the world reserve currency, the base of all transactions and especially of gold buying and selling, did not give a clean sweep. Russia, China and the Arab nations hold their own resources and physical gold and want to end the one-sided monopoly.
3. **Gold price control keeps the system afloat.** "The real fundamentals of the markets, stemming from XAU/USD." Rising spending weakens confidence in the USD through inflation. Competitors, China above all, can offer their own fundamentals-based gold price, and a higher price attracts investors and more physical gold accumulation. So the price has to be constantly adjusted ("manipulated") to allow only "completely accounted for changes".
4. **It all comes down to liquidity.** Fundamentals ultimately act on the physical supply of and demand for gold. If the banks don't move their fixing of XAU/USD to match physical demand in due time, other major countries and players win.

## What the banks do

- "Banks are always short-selling only to buy again at some later date."
- They would like to take price down forever but can't because of competition. Their role is to **manage the required price level** of gold and profit greatly while doing it.
- Their profit comes from controlling all bid and ask prices, with complete access to the exact order flow (the liquidity on both sides), enhanced with AI to target at the most macro level.

## Their limit, and why it matters to us

> There is an extent they are comfortable with before fundamental factors, namely for buys, overpower.

The banks can hold gold down only so far before fundamental buying pressure overrides them.

- The general day trader can't use this knowledge because they can't view the market "from the true lens of RR extraction".
- For us, it is **"the last step in securing the bank's complete footprint"**, and it supports **full swing confident holds**.
- The aim is to see the banks' "printing press of all the markets stemming from USD and then stemming from XAU control exactly as they do."

## On the chart: FU and the fractal approach

- "Every move of price action shown is via all markets' base programmed nature based on liquidity manipulation via FU." FU is the manipulated high/low; see [Task 1](/mrc-tasks/tasks/1/assignment/) and Task 2's objectives.
- FU is "our weapon to study the true market order flow from the naked eye."
- From it comes the **fractal approach** to extraction: scalp, intraday or swing. The link to XAU manipulation is always involved, so "we always follow the true value trace of price".

## Reading news against the chart

This is the key practical point from the timeline below. On the Nasrallah news:

> Although buys are already reflected in liquidity + overall setting, this incident made buys more important to prepare for market open and mitigation [of] new incidents over the weekend.

- **The bias comes from the liquidity read first.** The fundamental event reinforced a buy bias that was already in the chart. It did not create it.
- **Fundamentals raise conviction in holding.** Here the news made it more important to be positioned for buys into the market open and to cover new incidents over the weekend gap.
- The source gives **no fundamentals-based entry or exit rule** beyond this. Fundamentals support holds and bias; the chart (liquidity, FU) stays the basis.

## Worked example: 2023-24 events and gold

Mr Casino's recurring pattern: war and escalation mean more spending and inflation pressure on the USD, so gold must go higher. Each event below is paired with his stated reading of its effect. The readings are his interpretation, not verified cause and effect.

- **Oct 7th attacks, then Israel's assault on Gaza:** "We see gold price never returns to this date (that gap signifies their loss)." In his reading, a gap that price never returns to marks a loss for the banks.
- **War funding on top of the Ukraine/Russia conflict:** "Every bullet spent has its cost." Gold has to go higher to manage the inflation effect of all the spending on the USD.
- **Hezbollah joins from Lebanon:** further escalation.
- **Houthis (Yemen) disrupt shipping lanes:** everything becomes more expensive, with less supply of physical goods and more spending against them.
- **Global protests, with China and Russia highlighting what he calls the world's hypocrisy:** "The USD power is further questioned globally."
- **Iran directly attacks Israel for the first time:** major escalation risk. Attempts to pacify the situation worked that time.
- **Iran's president Raisi dies in a helicopter crash:** further tension.
- **Assassination attempt on Donald Trump:** tensions higher still. He promises more on this later.
- **CrowdStrike global IT outage (about a week later):** "The markets were certainly affected." He asks whether it was a test for a future event.
- **Hamas leader Haniyeh assassinated in Iran:** retaliation expected, which means heavy spending and an "instant rise in gold price".
- **An American/Turkish citizen killed in the West Bank:** Turkey threatens to get involved.
- **Pager and radio explosions in Lebanon:** "Global security is undermined. Gold prices go higher."
- **Nasrallah killed; open war with Lebanon:** "Gold price shooting up before market closure - the fundamental at play." See *Reading news against the chart* above.
