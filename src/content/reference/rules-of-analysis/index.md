## The rules

### 1. Leverage

> You do not need more than 1:200 leverage to correctly utilize the system. In fact, it is not recommended. Learn first to maximize quality.

### 2. Pure price action, detached

> Make your decisions based on pure price action from the lens of liquidity manipulation and mechanical TFS. You only improve this via a collected mind, backtesting, and its erasure of doubt. Learn to detach emotions—something that doesn't affect how price moves or increases your extraction (make it like the closed ended game of chess—you know all the rules).

The chess comparison is set out in full in [Trading Psychology](/mrc-tasks/misc/trading-psychology-the-mindset-of-clarity/#why-trading-is-psychology-intensive).

### 3. Wait for the established 10/15 min TS

> Follow, and wait for the established 10/15 min TS (HCS or negation) from POI. The average minimum manipulation to start the true move. This rule will have you know when to take a trade or watch price gathering liquidity details for that moment. Stick to it.

This restates [Task 3's rules](/mrc-tasks/tasks/3/assignment/#the-rules): "No 10 min + HCS / negation backing = no trade."

### 4. Know your liquidity targets first

> Know your liquidity targets before any entry. Not only that, account for liquidity manipulation first before the true move (early retail liquidity will be manipulated first). A full liquidity calculation (trust its fact - task 1).

"task 1": [Task 1 - Major Liquidity](/mrc-tasks/tasks/1/assignment/). On the worked example's 1 min charts: "Retail liquidity first manipulated" and "Early retail buys taken".

### 5. Core liquidity

> Core liquidity = most important (obvious/concentrated) liquidity that confirms direction for confident entry—that which you would like to hold minimum until. Something that finalizes bias.

The short definition is in [Terminology](/mrc-tasks/reference/terminology/#core-liquidity).

### 6. LAOL

> LAOL = refined liquidity area within POI (TS/TFS) where we expect reversal—our extended major targets. Price moves from LAOL to LAOL. We have scalp, intraday, and swing LAOL targets in this regard (we determine depending on TFS strength, core targets opposite, and new POI/TS respected - task 2).

"task 2": [Task 2 - Top-Down Analysis](/mrc-tasks/tasks/2/assignment/). Short definition in [Terminology](/mrc-tasks/reference/terminology/#laol).

*Scalp, intraday and swing LAOL carried down to the LTF entry: [The Top-Down Extraction Cycle · LAOL down the cycle](/mrc-tasks/reference/top-down-extraction-cycle/#laol-down-the-cycle).*

### 7. Refresh each timing

> Refresh liquidity calculation at the start of each new timing.

The timings are set out in [Task 3 · Key timing](/mrc-tasks/tasks/3/assignment/#3--key-timing).

### 8. TFS

> TFS tells you the power of the move/the current range of the price. Wait for the right quality-aligned manipulation and follow the direction of its TS build-up. 1hr/3hr/4hr/5hr/7hr/11hr backed generally gives a 300 pip + AVG move (true swing backed move - task 3).

"task 3": [Task 3 - Timeframe strength and the HTF true stop](/mrc-tasks/tasks/3/assignment/#2--timeframe-strength-and-the-htf-true-stop), which gives the TFS definition, the trade-type tiers and the "(3hr - 5hr - 7hr - 11hr TFS)" ladder.

Week 2 set out the full TFS framework: categories, established vs forming, the retest method: [Timeframe Strength (TFS)](/mrc-tasks/reference/timeframe-strength/).

### 9. Zones

> Zones are secondary confirmations, similar to an HCS POI—their reactions make the true move.

On the worked example's 4hr chart: "4hr HCS Zone reaction - secondary confluence that makes up POI".

*The full zone method (Week 1) is on [Zones: Types, Rules & Refinement](/mrc-tasks/reference/zones/).*

### 10. Final entries

> Your final entries are taken with confidence in liquidity direction, established 10 min TS, following TFS, in timing. Generally speaking, you will always have a refined HCS or negation POI, a LTF TS formed and respected (a strong entry model that occurred after previous liquidity was manipulated), LAOL taken, core + trail + LAOL to target.

Restated as a checklist [below](#entry-checklist).

### 11. Do not get caught up in entry models

> Do not get caught up in final entry models as many traders do, and forget the main theme—liquidity calculation. You have all that is required for a high level of extraction. Accept you won't be right every time. But every loss is due to a missing liquidity calculation, which you must aspire to figure out, not have confusion or think one entry model will fix it all (wasted energy). Focus instead on RR—One win makes you x10, x20, x50 any loss.

RR is the subject of [Task 3 · Risk to reward](/mrc-tasks/tasks/3/assignment/#1--risk-to-reward-and-the--compound).

*Segment 1 uses "HCS in lieu of entry model": [The Top-Down Extraction Cycle · HCS as the refinement](/mrc-tasks/reference/top-down-extraction-cycle/#hcs-as-the-refinement).*

### 12. Optimism and pessimism

He introduces this as a known saying ("Some of you will have heard this statement previously"):

> "Losing traders are optimistic in a losing position, and pessimistic in a winning position—winning traders are pessimistic in a losing position and optimistic in a winning position."

> Make the mindset shift to truly focus and maximize on RR.

The same mindset is developed in [Trading Psychology](/mrc-tasks/misc/trading-psychology-the-mindset-of-clarity/#risk-management-rr-and-probabilities).

## Entry checklist

*Editorial restatement of rule 10, in his order. Rule 10 above is the authority.*

Before a final entry:

- Confidence in liquidity direction
- Established 10 min TS
- Following TFS
- In timing
- A refined HCS or negation POI
- An LTF TS formed and respected (a strong entry model after previous liquidity was manipulated)
- LAOL taken
- Targets: core + trail + LAOL

His own compressed version, written on the worked example's 4hr chart as the "Backtest sequence": "HCS zone reaction / TFS / LAOL met / TS respected / 10 min TS EST / core + major + LAOL to target / timing".
