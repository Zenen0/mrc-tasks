## How the two Tasks fit

These are the "two ways we use TFS". Task 1 practises the **established** use: the retest of the established TFS POI, 3hr to 30 min. Task 2 practises the **forming** use: the swing power POI forming from zones across the higher timeframes, followed by the established TFS retests beneath it. Task 2 builds on the HTF zone marking of [Week 1 Task 1](/mrc-tasks/tasks/weeks/1/task-1/assignment/).
