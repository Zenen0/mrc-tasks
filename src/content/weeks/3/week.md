## How it builds

Each Week has added one layer to the same chart: [zones](/mrc-tasks/reference/zones/) in [Week 1](/mrc-tasks/tasks/weeks/1/) (where the banks' orders sit), [timeframe strength](/mrc-tasks/reference/timeframe-strength/) in [Week 2](/mrc-tasks/tasks/weeks/2/) (the direction and force of the move released from them), and [liquidity](/mrc-tasks/reference/liquidity/) in Week 3 ("where orders exist to be consumed ,hunted, or used for movement"). The liquidity he marks as "basic" is the unmanipulated doji and the big wick to fill of [standalone Task 1](/mrc-tasks/tasks/1/assignment/#the-30-chart-task).

He closed the Week with:

"But this comprehensive outlook of zones, TFS and liquidity - you will use in your daily backtesting- really reading banks intentions and thinking about the true premium areas to enter alongside them. Primed now for an extra ordinary potential of RR extraction."
